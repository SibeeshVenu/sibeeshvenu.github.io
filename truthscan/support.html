🚀 Quick Start Guide

🤫

Step 1

Set Up Secretly

Open TruthScan, choose a scenario (e.g. "Did you brush your teeth?"), and select the result you want to reveal.

📱

Step 2

Hand to Child

Pass the phone to your child and ask them to press their finger on the glowing scan area. The scanning animation begins!

🎭

Step 3

Dramatic Reveal

The screen shows your pre-set result. Watch their reaction — then enjoy the conversation that follows!

Navigate to My Scenarios in the app, then tap + Add. You can set a custom question, choose the result to reveal, and add personalised messages. Adding custom scenarios requires TruthScan Pro. Free users can browse the 5 pre-seeded samples and 5 built-in presets.
With TruthScan Pro, you can export and import your scenarios:
  1. On your old device, go to Settings → Export Scenarios. This creates a JSON file you can share or save.
  2. On your new device, install TruthScan, unlock Pro (restore your purchase — see below), then go to Settings → Import Scenarios and select the JSON file.
Your purchase is tied to your App Store or Google Play account. To restore it:
  1. Open TruthScan and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Restore Purchases.
  3. Your Pro status will be restored automatically as long as you are signed in with the same App Store / Google Play account used for the original purchase.
