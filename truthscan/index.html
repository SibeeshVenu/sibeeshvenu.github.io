Getting Started

How It Works

1
🤫

Parent Sets Up Secretly

Pick a scenario — like "Did you brush your teeth?" — and choose the reveal result. Only you know what the scan will show.

2
📱

Hand It to Your Child

Pass the phone over and ask them to place their finger on the screen. The dramatic scanning animation begins.

3
🎭

Dramatic Scan Reveal

The screen displays your preset result — "TRUTH DETECTED" or "FIB DETECTED" — sparking conversations, laughs, and confessions!
