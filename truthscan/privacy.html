Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 2025  ·  Last updated: November 2025

🛡️ Zero Data Collection

TruthScan does not collect, transmit, or store any personal, biometric, or health data — by design.

1 Overview

TruthScan ("the App") is a novelty parenting conversation game developed by Sibeesh Venu. This policy explains our approach to privacy, which is straightforward: we collect nothing.

TruthScan is a roleplay toy. It does not perform any biometric scanning, health measurement, lie detection, or biological analysis of any kind. All "scan results" are pre-configured by the parent. The app is designed from the ground up to be safe, ethical, and completely private.

2 Data We Collect

We collect no personal data whatsoever. No names, email addresses, device identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports, or any other form of personal information are collected or transmitted.

All scenario configurations you create are stored exclusively on your own device. This data is never transmitted to any server, and we have no access to it.

3 Local Storage

The App uses AsyncStorage — a local, on-device key-value store provided by React Native — to persist the following data entirely on your device:

  • Your custom scan scenarios (labels, messages, and results)
  • Your Pro purchase status (a local flag set after a verified purchase)
  • App preferences and settings

This data exists only on the device where the App is installed. It is not synced to any cloud service, not shared with any third party, and not accessible to us.

Deleting your data: Uninstalling the App permanently removes all locally stored data from your device.

4 In-App Purchases

TruthScan offers an optional "Pro" upgrade (€1.99, one-time lifetime purchase) processed exclusively by:

  • Apple App Store (iOS devices)
  • Google Play Store (Android devices)

All payment processing is handled entirely by Apple or Google. We never see, receive, or store your payment details, card information, or billing address. Only a local flag confirming a valid purchase is saved on your device via AsyncStorage.

Apple's and Google's privacy policies govern how they handle payment data during the purchase process.

5 Children's Privacy

TruthScan is designed for use by families with children aged approximately 4–12. Because we collect no personal data whatsoever, the App is compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

We do not knowingly collect any information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no registration, no sign-in, and no data transmission.

6 GDPR (European Users)

Because TruthScan processes no personal data, our obligations under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are minimal. Specifically:

  • No personal data is collected, so there is nothing to request, export, or erase via our systems.
  • EU users exercise their right to erasure by simply uninstalling the App — this permanently deletes all locally stored data.
  • No data processing agreements are required, as we do not process personal data.

7 Third-Party Services

TruthScan does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or crash-reporting SDKs.

The only third-party interactions are:

  • Apple App Store — when processing an in-app purchase on iOS
  • Google Play Store — when processing an in-app purchase on Android

These platforms have their own privacy policies which govern their data practices independently of TruthScan.

8 Changes to This Policy

If we ever change our privacy practices, we will update this page with a new effective date. Because we collect no data, significant changes are unlikely. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

9 Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please get in touch via the contact page at sibeeshvenu.com.
